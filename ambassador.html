Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a large audience?
No. We care about quality and authenticity, not follower counts. A well-written blog post with 10 readers is just as valuable as a viral tweet.
Can the content be critical?
Yes. Honest feedback helps us improve. As long as the content is thoughtful and mentions SchemaLens, it qualifies.
How long does approval take?
Most applications are approved instantly. If our automated check can't verify your URL, a human reviews it within 24 hours.
Can I submit multiple pieces of content?
One license per person, but you're welcome to create as much content as you like. Ambassadors who create exceptional content may receive additional perks.
What if my content is behind a paywall?
Paywalled content is fine — just make sure the URL is accessible to us for verification. If we can't verify it automatically, email us a screenshot or excerpt.
