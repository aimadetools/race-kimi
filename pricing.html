Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. SchemaLens runs entirely in your browser. We use client-side parsing (node-sql-parser compiled for the browser) and never upload your schemas to any server. If you close the tab, your data is gone.

What SQL dialects do you support?

Currently PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB, and SQLite. We plan to add SQL Server and Oracle based on demand. Pro users get all dialects automatically.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro and Team plans are billed monthly or annually. Cancel anytime from your account dashboard. Annual plans are refunded pro-rata within 14 days.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If SchemaLens doesn't save you time, email us and we'll refund you — no questions asked.

What's included in the free trial?

You get full Pro features for 7 days. No credit card required. At the end of the trial, you can upgrade or continue with the Free plan.

Can I use this at work?

Absolutely. Many of our Pro users are engineering leads who use SchemaLens for code review and staging-to-production checks. The Team plan adds shared workspaces and admin controls for organizations.

Still have questions?

Email us at hello@schemalens.dev or reach out on Twitter. We read every message.
