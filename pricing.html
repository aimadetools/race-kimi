Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use SchemaLens in CI/CD for free?

Yes. The Free plan works with all CI/CD integrations — GitHub Action, GitLab CI, Jenkins, CircleCI, and Bitbucket Pipelines. You get full migration SQL, rollback scripts, and breaking change alerts on every pull request. Upgrade to Team for shared workspaces, drift alerts, and admin controls.

Is my data safe?

Yes. The core diff engine runs entirely in your browser or CI runner. We built a custom SQL parser in vanilla JavaScript and never upload your schemas to any server. If you close the tab, your data is gone.

What SQL dialects do you support?

PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB, SQLite, SQL Server, and Oracle. All dialects are available on every plan, including Free.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro and Team plans are billed monthly or annually. Cancel anytime from your Gumroad account. Annual plans are refunded pro-rata within 14 days.

What is the Lifetime plan?

Lifetime Pro is a one-time payment of $39. You get all Pro features forever — unlimited tables, migration generation, exports, and every future Pro update. No recurring bills. Best for developers who hate subscriptions.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If SchemaLens doesn't save you time, email us and we'll refund you — no questions asked.

What can I do on the Free plan?

The Free plan includes unlimited schema diff, full migration SQL generation, rollback scripts, ORM export, Markdown/PDF/JSON export, and all 5 SQL dialects. It also works with our free GitHub Action and CI/CD integrations. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited saved diff history, shareable links, access to all 80+ micro-tools, and priority support.

Can I try Pro before buying?

The web diff is free forever with no table limits. You can try Pro features (exports, history, share links) free for 24 hours in the app. No email or credit card required.

Can I use this at work?

Absolutely. SchemaLens is built for engineering teams who review database changes in CI/CD. The Team plan adds shared workspaces, Slack/Teams drift alerts, a team risk dashboard, and admin controls.
