FAQ

What if my school doesn't use .edu?

We accept many international domains (.ac.uk, .edu.au, .edu.cn, .fr, .de, .nl, etc.). If your accredited institution email isn't accepted, contact us at hello@schemalens.tech and we'll add it manually.

Can I use this for my internship or job?

Yes. The license is yours personally. Use it for coursework, side projects, internships, and your first job.

What happens when I graduate?

Nothing. The license is lifetime. You keep Pro forever, even after graduation.

I'm a bootcamp student. Do I qualify?

Yes, if your bootcamp provides you with an accredited institution email. Otherwise, email us with proof of enrollment and we'll review it.

Is the license really lifetime?

Yes. You get the same Lifetime Pro license we sell for $39 (or $9 during the Race to the Finish). All future updates included.

Can I share my license?

No. One license per student. Sharing will invalidate the key. But tell your classmates about this page — they can get their own free license.
