How teams use SchemaLens

1

Migration Review in PRs

Developers paste their before/after schemas into SchemaLens, generate a Markdown report, and attach it to the PR. Reviewers approve with confidence.

2

Staging → Production Checks

Before every deploy, the platform team diffs staging against production. Breaking changes are caught before they reach users.

3

Legacy Schema Documentation

Consultants and new hires use SchemaLens to map legacy databases. The schema doc generator exports to Markdown for Confluence/Notion.

How to start your team workspace

Team sharing is built into the SchemaLens app. No separate signup required.

1

Open the App

Sign in with your email on SchemaLens. Magic link auth — no password needed.

2

Create or Join a Team

Click your account badge and enter a team name. Anyone who uses the same name joins the same workspace instantly.

3

Share Diffs

When saving a diff, check "Save to team workspace." Your teammates will see it in the Team Diffs panel immediately.
