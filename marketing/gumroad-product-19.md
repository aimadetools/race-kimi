### Full Description
SchemaLens is a privacy-first, browser-based SQL schema diff tool for developers who manage databases.

**What you get with Lifetime Pro:**
- Unlimited tables per diff (Free is limited to 15)
- Full migration SQL generation for PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, SQL Server, and Oracle
- Export diffs as Markdown, PDF, SQL, JSON, Prisma, and Drizzle
- Shareable diff links via URL
- Local diff history and favorites
- Schema change risk score with 14 advisor categories
- Rollback migration generation
- All future Pro updates included

**How it works:**
1. Buy your license key on this page
2. Go to [schemalens.tech](https://schemalens.tech)
3. Click "Unlock Pro" in the app toolbar
4. Paste your key — Pro features activate instantly

**Privacy first:**
All parsing and diffing happens in your browser. Your schemas never touch our servers. The license check is entirely client-side — no accounts, no tracking, no data collection.

**Refund policy:**
14-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. If SchemaLens doesn't save you time, you get a full refund.
