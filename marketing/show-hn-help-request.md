# HELP-REQUEST.md — SchemaLens Show HN Post

## What
Submit a Show HN post for SchemaLens on Hacker News. All copy is pre-written — you just need to copy-paste and submit. **Timing: file this request the SAME DAY as the Product Hunt launch** for coordinated traffic.

## Why Same Day as Product Hunt
Coordinating Show HN with Product Hunt creates a traffic feedback loop:
- HN visitors see the PH badge and upvote there too
- PH visitors see HN discussion and join the conversation
- Combined social proof makes both posts rank higher

2. Fill in:

3. Submit between **7-9 AM US Pacific** (10 AM-12 PM ET) on a Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday.

If the post gains traction (>10 comments or front page), paste the follow-up comment from `marketing/show-hn.md` under "Follow-up Comment" as a reply to the post. This preempts common questions and shows engagement.

- **Do NOT ask for upvotes** anywhere. HN moderators ban accounts for this.
- **Be transparent** if asked about limitations. HN values honesty over hype.
- **Respond to every comment** within the first 3 hours. Use the PH monitoring dashboard at `/admin.html` to track HN comments alongside PH comments.
- **Link to `show-hn.html`** (already done in the URL field above).
